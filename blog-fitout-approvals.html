FIT-OUT

Required approvals for commercial fit-outs

Understand the NOC permits required from Dubai Municipality, DEWA, and Civil Defense before starting false ceiling or partitioning works.

Navigating the regulatory landscape in Dubai is a crucial first step for any commercial fit-out project. Undertaking unauthorized modifications to an office or retail space can result in severe penalties and force you to reverse the changes at your own cost.

Key Approvals Required

1. Dubai Municipality (DM): Required for almost all structural changes, flooring, and ceiling works.
2. Dubai Civil Defense (DCD): Mandatory for any modifications that affect fire safety systems, sprinklers, and emergency exits.
3. DEWA: Necessary when altering the electrical load or plumbing connections of the unit.
