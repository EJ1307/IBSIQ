Smart Tips

Smart Maintenance Advice

AC GUIDE

How often should you wash AC filters?

In Dubai's dusty weather, AC filters should be washed every 2 weeks to maintain air quality and optimize electrical efficiency.

FIT-OUT

Required approvals for commercial fit-outs

Understand the NOC permits required from Dubai Municipality, DEWA, and Civil Defense before starting false ceiling or partitioning works.

PLUMBING

Hidden signs of wall water leaks

Peeled paint and low tap water pressure are key signs of a copper pipe leak hidden inside brick walls. Contact plumbing support immediately.
